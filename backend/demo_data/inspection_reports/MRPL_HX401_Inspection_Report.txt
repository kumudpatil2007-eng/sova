3. VISUAL & METALLURGICAL OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Severe localized pitting corrosion detected along the bottom quadrant of Pass 2 tubes.
2. Micro-cracking and chloride stress corrosion cracking (SCC) visible around tube-to-tubesheet seal welds.
3. Heavy scale deposition and under-deposit corrosion observed under sulfur-rich sludge build-up.
4. Calculated annualized corrosion rate: 0.95 mm/year (significantly exceeding historical 0.15 mm/year baseline).

4. INTEGRITY SUMMARY & RISK ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The measured tube wall thickness of 3.18 mm has breached the mandatory minimum safety limit of 3.50 mm specified in MRPL Safety SOP-08. Immediate risk of tube rupture leading to hydrocarbon contamination into cooling stream.
